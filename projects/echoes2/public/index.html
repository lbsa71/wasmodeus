How to play Capture coral. Learn from the past.

Charge a blue cell, or claim an empty cell beside blue. Each click adds one charge. At four, the cell bursts: it sends a charge up, right, down and left, capturing those cells for blue. That can set off another burst. Charge that spills over the board’s edge disappears.

Capture every coral cell to win. After 16 of your moves, the side controlling more cells wins. Equal territory is a draw.

The ghost plays coral using moves you made in earlier matches. It recognises local patterns, including rotations and reflections. If it cannot remember a reply, it passes and you play again. A newly discovered unanswered position earns +1 breakthrough.

Your first match teaches the ghost. Later matches can begin at a position the ghost could not answer, with colours reversed: your next decision teaches it what was missing. Every turn saves the entire archive.
