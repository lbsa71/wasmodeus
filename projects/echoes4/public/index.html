How the food chain works

Use the left stick to swim; ease the stick back to slow down. A starts or resumes. Menu pauses. View opens tuning; D-pad selects and adjusts sliders, B returns. X records the current dive while paused. Y shows upcoming meals.

You grow with every meal. Bigger fish swim faster and turn more slowly. There is no hunger in this experiment.

A ghost keeps swimming until it reaches a meal that can no longer happen. Then it stops and becomes passive food.

Save a fish from its recorded predator and it swims to the end of its recording. New collisions between any fish create new meals.
