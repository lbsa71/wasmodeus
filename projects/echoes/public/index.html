Make four. Outwit the past.

Make a straight line of all four blue stones, horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Four amber stones in a line wins for the ghost—even if you complete it.

You and the ghost can move either colour. Move one cell, including diagonals; keep all eight stones connected along their edges. The ringed stone rests for one turn. Colours stay with their stones.

The ghost chooses among earlier human moves, rotated or reflected to fit. It tries to win, block your next win, or build a threat. If it has no recorded reply, you get an extra move. A new frontier also earns a discovery point.

Discoveries keep the match going. Four in a row wins it. Matches restart automatically; after 24 of your moves without a line, the match is a draw.
